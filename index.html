How it works

  • Connect your analog synth to an audio interface input, press START and pick the interface and channel. Play single notes: the rig tracks the pitch and the synth's own envelope.
  • Well tempered: each note snaps to the nearest note of your key and scale in the chosen temperament (Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti, Young II or equal), with A at your reference. SNAP sets how hard it pulls; BASS RANGE folds everything into E1–D#3; OCTAVE shifts it.
  • Bass voice: morphing saw / square / pulse oscillator with a sub, wavefolder, grit, ladder low-pass with envelope, and drive. SYNTH IN blends your original synth back in.
  • 3D pad: X, Y and Z each control a parameter you choose. Drag in the cube for X/Y; use the Z fader, wheel or a two-finger pinch for Z. ORBIT and QUBIT move the point for you.
  • Pre-amp EQ: drag the points (left/right = frequency, up/down = gain) to shape the bass before the amp. LOW CUT removes rumble.
  • Modulation: two LFOs (sine, triangle, saw, square, sample & hold, smooth random) can wobble cutoff, pitch, volume, fold, shape, drive, resonance or sub. SYNC locks them to the BPM. The MOD effect switches between chorus, flanger, vibrato, phaser, rotary, ring mod and autopan.
  • IR sim: the last effect before the master. Pick a built-in room, plate, hall, spring, bass cab, radio or phone, or load any IR (.wav).
  • Input problems: the INPUT panel shows exactly what went wrong (blocked, busy, not found). Fix it and press RETRY INPUT.
  • Wet · dry · wet: the bass stays dry and mono in the centre. Delay, reverb, chorus and doubler are high-passed wet sends on the sides.
  • 3D monitor: the spectrum history in perspective, coloured by the kind of sound: tonal, hum, or white / pink / brown / blue / violet noise. Drag it to tilt.
  • Feedback: output starts muted; PHONE SAFE adds echo cancellation and a volume cap; the feedback guard notches any ringing frequency.
  • Touch: knobs turn with a left/right swipe so vertical swipes still scroll. Double-tap resets. On phones, use the tabs at the bottom.
