How it works

  • Start: press ▶ START AUDIO and allow microphone access. Pick your audio interface and channel (IN 1 / IN 2 / 1+2). Use headphones.
  • Parallel mixer: your input feeds every branch at once (Direct, Amp, Drive, Delay, Reverb, Mod, Synth). Each branch has its own fader, pan, mute and solo, and they all sum into the master.
  • Controls: drag knobs up/down (or sideways), use the mouse wheel, or the arrow keys. Double-click or double-tap resets to default. Tap anywhere on a fader track to jump there.
  • NAM + IR: tap the AMP strip name, then load a .nam model and a cab IR, or use the starter models or TONE3000.
  • Synth: follows the pitch of single notes you play or sing, and turns them into a filtered synth voice.
  • EQ: drag the numbered points: left/right sets frequency, up/down sets gain. Wheel or the Q knob sets width.
  • Auto mix: Auto level balances the branches by loudness. Scenes store two mixes and morph between them. Automation records your moves and loops them.
  • Display: SCOPE shows the live waveform (input in grey behind the output). ROLL scrolls it over time. SPECTRUM shows frequencies. STRIP shows the selected branch.
  • Keys: Space starts audio, then toggles the monitor on and off (feedback panic).
