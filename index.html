How it works

  • Start, allow audio input and pick your interface and channel. The output stays muted until you confirm headphones.
  • Feedback guard watches the output. When a frequency starts to ring, it cuts it with a narrow notch, and briefly ducks the master if the ring gets loud. Notches show as dashed lines on the left display.
  • Pre-amp: noise gate, then autotune, then a 4-band EQ with low cut, all before the amp. The autotune snaps to the nearest note of your key and scale in the chosen temperament. Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti and Young II are historical well temperaments; A stays at your reference pitch.
  • Wet · dry · wet: the dry amp sits in the centre. Delay, reverb, chorus and doubler are 100% wet sends on the sides. Set DRY and WET separately; WIDTH and W/D/W control how far apart they sit.
  • End rack: add tremolo, auto filter, bitcrush, lo-fi radio, stutter or looper at the end of the chain. Reorder with ◀ ▶.
  • Qubit chaos: a simulated two-qubit quantum state moves the parameters you pick. ENTROPY sets how often the state is measured (it jumps); ENTANGLE links the two qubits; DEPTH sets how far it pushes.
  • Controls: drag knobs up/down or sideways, or use the wheel or arrow keys. Double-tap resets. Tap a fader track to jump there.
